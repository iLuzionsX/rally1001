RALLY RECORDED-AUDIO SOURCES — 2026-10-08
===========================================

The engine mixer uses real-world recordings only. It does not synthesize engine
or tire noise. The looping RPM/throttle segments are crossfaded samples cropped
from the recordings listed below. All sounds remain their creators' recordings.

Technical note: The recorded MP3s above are fetched from their public hosts by
the browser after the player enables audio. External hosts must remain reachable
and allow browser CORS. If an engine recording cannot load, the engine stays
silent rather than imitating it with oscillators.
